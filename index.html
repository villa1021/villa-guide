01 · Arrive

Finding your way in

Using GPS?
Apple Maps: Use the property address.
Google Maps & Waze: Search for “Villas at Norwich Inn.”

From Route 32

  1. Drive into the entrance marked “The Spa at Norwich Inn.”
  2. After passing the spa on your left, turn right down toward the villas.
  3. Continue straight, passing a small mail house on your right and a clubhouse on your left.
  4. Turn left after the clubhouse into the parking lot.

From New London Turnpike

  1. Enter through the gate marked “Private Community.” The gate code is 3-5-0-0.
  2. At the third stop sign, turn right, just before the clubhouse.

Parking & finding your unit

Find “Villa Ten” at the end of the lot and park anywhere.

Villa Ten · Second floor · Unit 1021
Go up either staircase. You’ll find unit 1021 in the corner on the second floor.

Unlocking the front door

View door-entry guide ↗
  1. Press the small circle on the right side of the handle to wake up the touchscreen.
  2. Enter your six-digit guest code.
  3. Press 🔒 to finish. Don’t skip this last step!

The door handle will automatically lock after a few seconds.

Having trouble getting in?
Please ring the doorbell camera for help accessing the villa.

If you’d like the doorbell camera turned off for your stay, please reach out.

02 · Access

Clubhouse location & entry

From Villa Ten, walk back toward the entrance to your parking lot. Look for the clubhouse you passed on arrival, beside the main road.

Bring the white clubhouse keycard from the counter. Use it to enter through either the front door or the side gate.

View clubhouse photos & entry guide ↗

The white clubhouse keycard is attached to a smaller keycard.

The smaller keycard opens the front door in place of your code. Wave it in front of the number 4 on the keypad.

Clubhouses are open daily from 8am to 10pm.

04 · Get comfortable

Using the thermostat

  1. Touch the screen to wake it up.
  2. Choose heat or cool using the symbol toward the top.
  3. Select your desired temperature.
  4. Choose how long you’d like the unit to run: 2 hours, 4 hours, or indefinitely.
Heading out? Please set the thermostat to heat mode at 60°F when leaving.
05 · Set the mood

Sonos speakers

A dedicated iPad mini controls the speakers. You’ll find it in the right drawer of the desk.

Apple Music & Spotify are already linked.

If you disconnect the charger, please plug it back into the iPad when you’re finished.

Please respect quiet hours: 10pm to 8am.
06 · Your coffee ritual

Nespresso

Pods and mugs are in the cabinet above the stove.

  1. Fill the water tank on the left with filtered water.
  2. Turn the knob on top counterclockwise to open.
  3. Place a pod inside, then push down while turning clockwise until it locks.
  4. The button should light up. Press it once to start brewing.
07 · A little self-care

Dyson Airwrap

Please read all instructions before use and clean thoroughly afterward.

The Airwrap is sanitized after each guest. Please remove all hair from the attachments before returning them to the case.

08 · Cozy up

Fireplace

Before starting a fire, the lever must be in the open position.

Where to find firewood

For good-quality firewood, we recommend Big Y in Norwich and Stop & Shop in Uncasville.

09 · Until next time

Before You Leave

Thank you for staying at Villa 1021. A few quick checks before you go:

  • ✓Thermostat: Set to heat mode at 60°F.
  • ✓Sonos iPad: Reconnect the charger if you disconnected it.
  • ✓Keycards: Leave the clubhouse keycard and smaller door keycard together on the counter.
  • ✓Fireplace: Make sure the fire is completely extinguished, with no glowing embers, before leaving. Never leave a fire unattended.
  • ✓Front door: Close the door and confirm it is locked before you go.
